# WooCommerce administration and customer handover

[← All production stories](../README.md)

`WooCommerce` `2,000+ products` `Customer training` `Operations`

## Situation

A bicycle retailer required a WooCommerce platform containing more than 2,000 products, while the business owner had limited previous experience using computer systems.

## Task

I needed to establish the initial catalogue and workflows, prepare the customer to operate the store, and continue supporting the production platform.

## Action

I personally configured the first 200 products and worked across products, variations, inventory, orders, payments, refunds, taxes, shipping, coupons, and customer accounts. I then delivered practical training tailored to the customer's technical level and provided continuing updates and troubleshooting.

## Result

The customer gained the ability to manage routine catalogue operations independently while retaining reliable technical support for more complex changes and incidents.

## CV-ready summary

> Supported a 2,000+ product WooCommerce platform, personally configuring the first 200 products and core commerce workflows before training a previously non-technical customer to manage routine catalogue operations independently.
