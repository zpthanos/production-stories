## 1. Situation

A bicycle retailer required a WooCommerce platform containing more than 2,000 products, while the business owner had limited previous experience using computer systems.

## 2. Objective

Establish the initial catalogue and workflows, prepare the customer to operate the store and continue supporting the production platform.

## 3. What I did

- Personally configured the first 200 products.
- Worked across variations, inventory, orders, payments, refunds, taxes, shipping, coupons and customer accounts.
- Delivered practical training tailored to the customer's technical level.
- Continued providing updates and troubleshooting support.

## 4. Result

The customer gained the ability to manage routine catalogue operations independently while retaining reliable technical support for more complex changes and incidents.

## Recruiter takeaway

> Supported a 2,000+ product WooCommerce platform, personally configuring the first 200 products and core commerce workflows before training a previously non-technical customer to manage routine catalogue operations independently.
