## 1. Situation

Kooba Beach originally requested a presentation website for its hospitality business.

## 2. Objective

Make the website useful during the customer's day-to-day guest experience.

## 3. What I did

- Recommended adding digital catalogues and menus.
- Incorporated those materials into the website.
- Enabled the business to connect physical QR codes directly to the relevant pages.

## 4. Result

Guests can access current menus through their phones, extending the website from a promotional presence into a practical customer-service channel.

## Recruiter takeaway

> Increased the operational value of Kooba-Beach.gr by proposing and implementing digital menus connected to on-site QR codes, turning a presentation website into a practical guest-service channel.
