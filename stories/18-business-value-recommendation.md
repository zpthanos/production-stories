# Proactive business-value recommendation

[← All production stories](../README.md)

`Hospitality` `QR menus` `Consulting` `Customer experience`

## Situation

[Kooba Beach](https://www.kooba-beach.gr/) originally requested a presentation website for its hospitality business.

## Task

I identified an opportunity to make the website useful during the customer's day-to-day guest experience.

## Action

I recommended adding digital catalogues and menus, incorporated them into the website, and enabled the business to connect physical QR codes directly to the relevant pages.

## Result

Guests can access current menus through their phones, extending the website from a promotional presence into a practical customer-service channel.

## CV-ready summary

> Increased the operational value of Kooba-Beach.gr by proposing and implementing digital menus connected to on-site QR codes, turning a presentation website into a practical guest-service channel.
