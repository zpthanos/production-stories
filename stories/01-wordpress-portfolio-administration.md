[← All production stories](../README.md)

`WordPress` `WooCommerce` `Production operations` `Access control`

## Situation

I was responsible for supporting 60 production websites serving different business purposes, including 48 WordPress sites and 12 WooCommerce stores.

## Task

I needed to keep the portfolio secure, stable, and maintainable while controlling access and supporting ongoing customer changes.

## Action

I managed WordPress core, theme, and plugin updates; created and tested file and database backups; monitored Site Health; optimized databases; moderated spam; managed permalinks and SEO settings; and administered users, roles, password resets, and access restrictions.

## Result

The portfolio remained maintainable and ready for ongoing content, functionality, and ecommerce changes, with controlled access and repeatable maintenance practices.
