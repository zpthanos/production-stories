## 1. Situation

I was responsible for supporting 60 production websites serving different business purposes, including 48 WordPress sites and 12 WooCommerce stores.

## 2. Objective

Keep the portfolio secure, stable and maintainable while controlling access and supporting ongoing customer changes.

## 3. What I did

- Managed WordPress core, theme and plugin updates.
- Created and tested file and database backups.
- Monitored Site Health and optimized databases.
- Moderated spam and maintained permalinks and SEO settings.
- Administered users, roles, password resets and access restrictions.

## 4. Result

The portfolio remained maintainable and ready for ongoing content, functionality and ecommerce changes, with controlled access and repeatable maintenance practices.

## Recruiter takeaway

> Administer 60 production websites, including 48 WordPress sites and 12 WooCommerce stores, covering updates, tested backups, database optimization, user permissions, security controls, Site Health and ongoing maintenance.
