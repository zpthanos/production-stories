`AI-assisted work` · `Documentation` · `QA planning` · `Confidentiality`

> **Outcome:** Produced clearer documentation, training material and QA plans more efficiently while retaining source verification, confidentiality and final accountability.

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Tools** | ChatGPT and Claude |
| **Uses** | Documentation, task decomposition, training and QA planning |
| **Controls** | Source verification, manual correction and no confidential data |

## 1. Situation

Producing detailed developer documentation, customer training material and manual QA plans from unstructured technical notes required substantial organization and drafting effort.

## 2. Objective

Shorten preparation time while retaining technical accuracy, confidentiality and personal accountability.

## 3. What I did

- Used AI tools to organize verified notes and structure documentation.
- Broke complex requests into practical execution steps.
- Prepared manual QA checklists and summarized relevant release information.
- Compared every output with source documentation, logs and observed behaviour.
- Manually corrected deficiencies.
- Excluded client identities, credentials and confidential information.

## 4. Result

I produce clearer documentation, training material and testing plans more efficiently while retaining final responsibility for every technical conclusion and customer-facing output.

## Recruiter takeaway

> Use ChatGPT and Claude to accelerate developer documentation, customer training material, task decomposition and QA planning, validating every output against source evidence and excluding confidential client information.
