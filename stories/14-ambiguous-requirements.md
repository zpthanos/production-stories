## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Starting point** | “A website for my radio station that should refer to children” |
| **Approach** | Discovery, research, recommendations and iteration |
| **Role signal** | Requirements engineering · Consulting · Customer collaboration |

## 1. Situation

A customer initially requested only “a website for my radio station that should refer to children,” without defined functionality, structure or detailed requirements.

## 2. Objective

Turn the incomplete idea into a viable child-focused radio website while allowing the customer's requirements to develop.

## 3. What I did

- Interviewed the customer.
- Researched relevant EU considerations for child-oriented online content.
- Translated the findings into design and content recommendations.
- Created an initial tailored implementation.
- Used review and iteration to clarify the emerging requirements.

## 4. Result

The early concept gave the customer something concrete to evaluate, enabled clearer requirements and developed into the completed [RadioFamily.gr](https://www.radiofamily.gr/) platform.

## Recruiter takeaway

> Transformed an ambiguous request for a child-focused radio website into a structured delivery by eliciting requirements, researching applicable EU considerations and iterating from an initial tailored implementation to the completed platform.
