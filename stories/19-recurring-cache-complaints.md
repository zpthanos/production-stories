# Reducing recurring cache complaints

**Turning repeated customer frustration into a better support process**

`Voice of the customer` · `LiteSpeed` · `Cloudflare` · `Process improvement`

</div>

> **Outcome:** Improved cache-purging practices and created reusable guidance that reduced repeated confusion for customers and colleagues.

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Pattern** | Recently published changes not visible |
| **Systems** | Browser cache, LiteSpeed and Cloudflare |
| **Role signal** | Voice of the customer · Process improvement · Support enablement |

## 1. Situation

Multiple customers reported that recently published website changes were not visible because of browser, LiteSpeed or Cloudflare caching behaviour.

## 2. Objective

Address the technical cause while reducing repeated confusion and support effort.

## 3. What I did

- Analysed the recurring pattern across reports.
- Adjusted LiteSpeed caching methods.
- Configured Cloudflare cache purging where appropriate.
- Documented clear refresh and troubleshooting guidance for customers and team members.

## 4. Result

Updated content became available more reliably, while customers and colleagues gained a repeatable method for resolving or preventing similar reports.

## Recruiter takeaway

> Converted recurring customer feedback about stale content into improved LiteSpeed and Cloudflare cache-purging practices plus reusable troubleshooting guidance for customers and team members.
