## 1. Situation

MyKoinoxrista.eu initially requested a straightforward website presenting its business services.

## 2. Objective

Deliver greater operational value than a static company presentation.

## 3. What I did

- Analysed how prospective customers would interact with the business.
- Developed a custom form for advanced service-price calculation and booking.
- Created a custom archive structure for displaying business partners.

## 4. Result

The website became an interactive service and enquiry platform rather than only an online presentation.

## Recruiter takeaway

> Expanded a business-presentation brief for MyKoinoxrista.eu into an interactive service platform by developing a custom pricing and booking form and a structured partner-listing archive.
