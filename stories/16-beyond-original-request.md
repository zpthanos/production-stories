# Delivering beyond the original request

[← All production stories](../README.md)

`Consulting` `Custom development` `Booking` `Business analysis`

## Situation

[MyKoinoxrista.eu](https://www.mykoinoxrista.eu/) initially requested a straightforward website presenting its business services.

## Task

I wanted to deliver greater operational value than a static company presentation.

## Action

I analysed how prospective customers would interact with the business and developed a custom form for advanced service-price calculation and booking, together with a custom archive structure for displaying business partners.

## Result

The website became an interactive service and enquiry platform rather than only an online presentation.

## CV-ready summary

> Expanded a business-presentation brief for MyKoinoxrista.eu into an interactive service platform by developing a custom pricing and booking form and a structured partner-listing archive.
